    Questions

    Anything else: support@tradebay.io

    Is this the real platform?

    Yes. Trade Bay is an authorized reseller. You get the vendor's own build, delivered and kept current by the Trade Bay launcher.

    What does lifetime mean?

    You pay once and the licence does not expire. There is no renewal or subscription.

    How many computers can I use?

    A licence binds to the first PC you activate it on. If you replace that machine, email support and we'll reset the binding.

    Do I need to stay online?

    Only to activate and to download updates. After that, a cached, signed licence lets your platforms launch offline.

    Is market data included?

    No. Data feeds and broker connections are chosen and paid for separately, inside each platform, exactly as with a direct purchase.

    What do I need to run it?

    Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Each platform has its own hardware guidance; Sierra Chart is the lightest, ATAS X benefits most from a dedicated GPU.
